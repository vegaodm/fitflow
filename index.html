Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Applies to Spendly FitFlow for iPhone and iPad

Privacy Support How insights work

The short version. Spendly FitFlow has no account, no sign-in, no server and no analytics. Your meals, runs, nights, photos and voice notes are stored in one database file inside the app's own storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded, because the app has nowhere to upload it to.

We cannot see your data. There is no backend to see it from, and no account that would link it to you.

1What is stored, and where

Everything the app records lives on the device, in the app's own container:

What Where
Meals, foods, runs, routes, nights, weigh-ins, your personal targets One SQLite database file in the app's Application Support folder
Meal photos, voice notes, diagnostics text Documents/FitFlow — visible in the Files app, so you can reach your own files
Preferences (language, reminders, whether onboarding is done) iOS UserDefaults, on the device
The bedtime widget's Start/Stop timestamps A shared UserDefaults container readable only by the app and its own widget

None of this leaves the device. The app makes no network request of its own — there is no code in it that opens a connection to a server.

2Permissions, and why each one is asked for

  • Location (including while in use in the background). Used only while you are recording a run, to draw the route and compute distance, pace and elevation. It keeps recording with the screen locked so a run is not cut short. It is never used at any other time, and it is never sent anywhere.
  • Motion & fitness activity type. Only to tell iOS the location session is a workout, so it is not throttled.
  • Camera. Only when you choose to photograph a meal. The photo is saved to the app's own folder on the device.
  • Microphone. Only when you choose to attach a voice note to a meal entry. The recording is saved to the app's own folder on the device.
  • Notifications. Only for the optional bedtime reminder you can switch on. Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS.
  • Live Activities. Shows the current run's distance, pace and elapsed time on the Lock Screen.
  • Files access. Only when you export or import a backup file you picked yourself.

You can revoke any of these in iOS Settings at any time. The features that need them stop working; the rest of the app keeps working, and your existing data is untouched.

3The optional AI lookup — the one time data leaves the app

The food lookup screen offers to hand a question to an AI assistant. You choose which one, and you choose it in Settings. This is the only path by which anything you typed leaves the app, and it is opt-in every single time:

  • The app opens another app on your device — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot, Brave Search, Pocket LLM, Pocket LLM G or a service you configure — using a URL scheme, with your question in the link. It can also copy the question to the clipboard.
  • Spendly FitFlow makes no network request itself and stores no API key.
  • Once the question is handed over, the receiving service's own privacy policy applies, not this one. That service may be operated by a different company, in a different country, and may keep the question.
  • The reply is pasted back by you. The app parses it locally to fill in a food's nutrition; nothing is sent to verify it.

If you never use the lookup screen, the app never contacts anything.

4Purchases

Lifetime Membership is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. The transaction is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, your Apple Account, your payment method or your address. The app only receives from Apple a signed confirmation that the purchase exists, and keeps a local flag so it can draw the right screen offline.

Apple's handling of the purchase is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

5What we do not do

  • No account, no email address, no phone number collected.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifier, no attribution SDK.
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data sold or shared.
  • No HealthKit access. The app reads no health data and writes none.

The app's privacy manifest declares exactly one accessed API category — UserDefaults, reason code CA92.1 — and no collected data types.

6Backups, and where they go

Settings → Backup copies the whole database file to a location you pick in the Files app. That copy is yours: if you save it to iCloud Drive or another synced folder, it is then subject to that provider's terms, because you put it there. If you save it locally, it stays local.

The app also keeps short-lived copies in its own temporary folders while a backup is being written or restored, and clears them afterwards.

7Deleting your data

  • Delete individual records in the app: swipe a meal, a run or a night.
  • Delete everything in Settings → Data → Delete all data. This removes your meals, exercise, weigh-ins, runs, nights, photos, recordings and custom foods. The reference food library and your personal targets stay.
  • Delete the app. Removing Spendly FitFlow from the device deletes its container, and with it the database and every media file.

Because we hold nothing, there is no separate deletion request to make. There is nothing on our side to erase.

8Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and erase personal data held about you, and to object to its processing. In this app all of that data is already in your hands, on your device:

  • Access and export — Settings → Backup writes the whole database out.
  • Correct — edit any record in the app.
  • Erase — Settings → Data → Delete all data, or delete the app.
  • Object — you can use the app with no network access at all.

We do not process your personal data on any server, so there is no processing to object to beyond what happens on your own device.

9Children

Spendly FitFlow is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

10Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it stores or where it sends it, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the effective date above will change. Because the app has no account, we cannot email you about it — the current version of this page is always the one that applies.
